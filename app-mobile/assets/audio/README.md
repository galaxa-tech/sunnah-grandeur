# Adhan audio — source & license

All three files are real adhan recitations sourced from Wikimedia Commons
under licenses that permit redistribution in this app. None is an official
recording from Masjid Al-Haram or Masjid An-Nabawi — the in-app labels
reflect that (see `lib/models/adhan_settings.dart`).

- **adhan.mp3** — "Beautiful adhan.ogg", CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg
- **adhan_makkah.mp3** — "Call to prayer by Sabah Fakhry.mp3" (1985), public
  domain (PD-old / PD-US-expired).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Call_to_prayer_by_Sabah_Fakhry.mp3
- **adhan_madinah.mp3** — "The Adhan - Muslim Call to Prayer - Aaqib
  Azeez.mp3", CC BY-SA 4.0 — attribution: Aaqib Azeez.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Adhan_-_Muslim_Call_to_Prayer_-_Aaqib_Azeez.mp3

All three were re-encoded to MP3 (from their original Ogg/MP3 source) for
consistent, universal playback across Android, iOS, and web (Safari does not
support Ogg Vorbis).
