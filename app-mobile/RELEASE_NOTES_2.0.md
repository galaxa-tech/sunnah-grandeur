## Tester checklist (practising-Muslim review)
- Prayer times for your city match your local masjid (method + Asr madhab in Profile → Prayer method)
- Hijri date matches local announcement; use the ± adjustment on the Hijri calendar if not
- Qibla direction at home matches a known reference
- Quran text renders correctly (Uthmani script, harakat) in both Read tab and surah header
- Ruqyah passages play the correct verses
- Zakat: nisab values look right for today's gold/silver price
